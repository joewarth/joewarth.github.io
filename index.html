Home

I'm Joe, an Actuarial Data Scientist focused on Commercial P&C pricing and pricing modernization. I build production-grade pricing models, monitoring frameworks, dashboards, and deployment patterns that help insurers make better pricing and underwriting.

My modeling philosophy was earned in real insurance environments, where models have to survive messy data, actuarial review, product strategy, underwriting judgment, market pressure, regulatory scrutiny, implementation constraints, production deployment, and post-launch monitoring. That experience taught me that the best pricing models are not just technically strong- they are trusted, explainable, governed, adopted, monitored, and continuously improved.

I believe data quality is part of the model, not a prerequisite someone else handles. Credibility beats cleverness. A pricing model is not just a statistical artifact; it is part of a pricing operating model that has to connect segmentation strategy, underwriting execution, regulatory expectations, and portfolio performance. User feedback is not noise; it is model lifecycle intelligence. And insurance pricing should be managed continuously, because no model is ever finished.

This page highlights a sample of my work across production insurance modeling, pricing analytics, deployment, dashboards, monitoring, and model lifecycle management.

For full work history and details, please see my LinkedIn. Thanks for visiting, and I'm always happy to connect.
