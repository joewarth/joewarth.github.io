Home

I'm Joe, an Actuarial Data Scientist focused on Commercial P&C pricing. I build pricing models, monitoring tools, dashboards, and deployment patterns that support real-world decision-making across insurance organizations of different sizes and levels of modeling maturity.

My modeling philosophy was earned through experience. I have worked in environments where pricing models had to survive more than a validation metric: messy insurance data, actuarial review, product strategy, underwriter judgment, market pressure, regulatory scrutiny, production deployment, and post-launch monitoring. That experience taught me that the best pricing models are not just technically strong- they are trusted, explainable, monitored, and continuously improved.

I believe data work is not beneath the modeler; it is the model. Credibility beats cleverness. A pricing model is a business system, not just a math artifact. User feedback is model monitoring. And insurance pricing should be managed continuously, because no model is ever finished.

This page highlights a sample of my work across production insurance modeling, analytics, deployment, dashboards, and monitoring tools.

For full work history and details, please see my LinkedIn. Thanks for visiting, and I'm always happy to connect.
